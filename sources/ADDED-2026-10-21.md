# Source audit, 2026-10-21

Every link in the main content of Mike's NACEP pages (About Us, Accreditation Commission, Accreditation FAQ, Application Process & Resources, Standards for Quality, What Is Concurrent Enrollment) was checked against the PDFs in `sources/files/`.

## Why the colleges question failed
NACEP's FAQ answer to "What colleges ... sponsor accredited programs?" is only the word "here", linked to nacep.org/accredited-programs. The PDF of the FAQ page has no college names, and GUIDE.md had left the list out on purpose because it changes often. The instructions also told the agent never to name a college. Both are now changed.

## Known issues in NACEP's own data
- The list header says 136 programs at 132 institutions; its table has 135 rows at 131 institutions.
- The cycles sheet shows 2029-2030 "Re-Accredited Through 2034-2035 (5 years)"; re-accreditation is 7 years. The instructions tell the agent to flag this.
- "Indian State University" is NACEP's spelling in the formerly accredited list.
- The Accreditation Institutes page names October 18, 2026 as the next institute, which has passed.

## Keeping it current
The programs list and cycles change every year. Re-export them from the two Google Sheets (export as .xlsx) when NACEP publishes a new revision and replace the two PDFs.
